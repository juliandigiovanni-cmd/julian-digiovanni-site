abstract: "We analyze the effects of ETS policy shocks on global stock market returns at the country-industry level using linear and spatial autoregression models. Results indicate that markets respond negatively to rising carbon prices, but the impact is modest. Spillovers are largely confined to sectors linked to EU industries through intermediate goods trade. There is no evidence of broader effects. Thus, the effects of ETS on stock returns appear limited in both magnitude and geographic scope."
