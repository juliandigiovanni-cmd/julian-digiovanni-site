abstract: "This paper presents a multi-country, multi-sector New Keynesian model to examine the drivers of global inflation during the pandemic and recovery periods. Shocks propagate through international trade and production networks, leading to global demand-supply imbalances. Production complementarities generate widespread sectoral cost-push inflation despite heterogeneous labour supply shocks. Flexible exchange rate regimes absorb cross-country monetary policy differences, curbing global demand reallocation and the inflationary spillovers relative to fixed-rate regimes."
