abstract: "We quantify the role of global production linkages in explaining spillovers of U.S. monetary policy shocks on country-sector stock returns, using a structural spatial autoregression model consistent with open-economy production network theory. Network effects from global supply chains account for around 70 percent of the total impact, and a counterfactual removing global production linkages reduces the total influence of U.S. monetary policy shocks by about half."
